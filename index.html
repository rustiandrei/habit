Humane Habit Philosophy

Habit tracking without the shame

Most habit trackers punish you with broken streaks the moment life gets in the way. We built Habit Tracker with compassion, giving you tools to handle vacations, illnesses, and off-days with dignity.

🛡️

Streak Shields

Consistency earns protection: you earn 1 Streak Shield for every 7 days of consistency, banking up to 2. If you miss a day due to travel or an emergency, the shield automatically steps in and freezes the day. Your hard-earned streak survives intact.

Automatic & Deterministic
🌴

Vacation Mode

Taking a well-deserved holiday? Enable Vacation Mode with a single toggle. All scheduled habits are excused during your getaway, so you return refreshed without facing a graveyard of broken streaks.

Guilt-Free Rest
⚖️

Build & Quit Goals

Not all progress is additive. Set up Build habits (drink water, meditate, stretch) or Quit habits (screen time, caffeine, fast food) with upper limits, periodic quotas (e.g., 3x/week), and flexible schedules.

Flexible Scheduling
