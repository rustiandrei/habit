Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Habit Tracker: Planner Routine

At Habit Tracker: Planner Routine, we believe that your personal habits, daily routines, emotional reflections, and wellness notes are deeply private and belong strictly to you.

1. On-Device Local Storage (SwiftData)

All habit schedules, completion logs, notes, time trackers, and mood entries are stored strictly on your device using Apple’s native SwiftData database. We do not operate backend servers that store or process your personal habit data.

2. No Accounts & No Personal Identifiers

You do not need to register with an email address, password, phone number, or social media profile to use Habit Tracker. The app is completely functional from the moment you install it without transmitting personal identity details.

3. Apple Health (HealthKit) Integration

If you grant permission, Habit Tracker reads and writes data (such as mindful minutes, hydration volume, steps, workouts, sleep, and HRV) directly through Apple's HealthKit framework.

  • Your health data is never collected, copied, or sent to external servers or third parties.
  • HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining.
  • You can modify or revoke HealthKit permissions anytime in iOS Settings > Health > Habit Tracker.

4. Biometric App Lock (Face ID / Touch ID)

When you enable the App Lock feature, biometric verification is executed entirely on your device via Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework and Secure Enclave. Habit Tracker does not collect, receive, or store your biometric data.

5. Analytics & Advertisements

Habit Tracker contains zero third-party advertising SDKs and zero behavioral tracking scripts. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

6. Data Backup & User Control

Your habits are included in your standard iPhone or iPad backups (iCloud Backup or your computer). The app itself has no cloud sync. You can also export your habit history manually at any time or delete your data directly from within the app.

7. External Links & Document Access
